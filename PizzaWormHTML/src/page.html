First worm to eat 10 pizzas, or the one still alive, wins the round.

How it plays

The worm never stops. Eat pizzas to grow; a pizza's radius (5 to 12 px) is what it scores. Hit the frame, yourself or the other worm and it is over. Phones: hold the left or right half of the screen to steer, tap to confirm.

Rebuilt from the original DOS executable: game logic ported from the Turbo Pascal code, with the original pictures, 5×5 font, palette tricks and the Izzard module played by a small ProTracker replayer. The game was released as freeware.
